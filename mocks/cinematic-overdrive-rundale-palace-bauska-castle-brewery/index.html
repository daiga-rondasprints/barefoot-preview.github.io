Highlights

Rundāle Palace, the baroque masterpiece by Rastrelli (architect of the Winter Palace in St Petersburg), with 40 restored staterooms and 10 hectares of French formal gardens.
Bauska Castle, the dramatic ruins and restored ducal residence of the Dukes of Courland, at the meeting of two rivers.
The Great Ķemeri Bog boardwalk, a flat loop on wooden boards through one of the best-preserved raised bogs in the Baltics.
The Duchy of Courland story, the small Baltic duchy that once held colonies in Tobago and the Gambia, told across the palace and the castle that were its great monuments.
Small group, up to 6 guests, with central Riga pickup and an air-conditioned vehicle for the whole day.

What to expect

My hope is to give you a carefully curated day in Latvia, personalised for our small group and well worth your ten hours. Rundāle Palace is the baroque masterpiece of the Baltics, designed by Bartolomeo Rastrelli, the architect of the Winter Palace in St Petersburg, for the Dukes of Courland. Forty of its rooms are fully restored, and the French formal gardens hold more than 2,300 rose varieties.

After the palace and its gardens, we drive a few minutes to Bauska Castle, where the ruins of the Livonian Order fortress stand beside the restored residence of the Dukes of Courland, at the confluence of the Mūsa and Mēmele rivers. Then we turn north for the Great Ķemeri Bog, where a flat wooden boardwalk loops out across one of the largest and best-preserved raised bogs in the Baltics, with dark pools, dwarf pines, and sphagnum moss underfoot the whole way.

Lunch is a sit-down meal at a country restaurant near Bauska, where you order and pay the restaurant directly. I bring a small Latvian tasting in the van, drinks, snacks and seasonal sweets, with the story behind each. Between stops there is folk music and the kind of conversation that makes a long day feel short.

Good to know

Best season

Year-round. The rose gardens peak from late June into July, the bog is striking in autumn and under winter frost, and the palace interiors are climate-controlled and rewarding in any weather.

Accessibility

The palace ground floor and gardens are largely step-free and the Ķemeri boardwalk is flat. Bauska Castle has uneven medieval stone and a tower climb, so tell us your needs and we plan around them. Not suitable for guests who cannot manage any stairs at all.

Frequently asked questions

How long is the excursion?

About 10 hours door to door. You spend the better part of the morning at Rundāle Palace and its gardens, then Bauska Castle, a sit-down lunch near Bauska, and a flat boardwalk loop into the Great Ķemeri Bog. We leave Riga in the morning and you are back in the early evening.

Is lunch included?

Lunch is a sit-down meal at a country restaurant near Bauska, and you order and pay the restaurant directly, so it is not part of the €94 tour price. Simple Latvian country cooking. Vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free and most allergies can be handled if you tell us at least 48 hours before departure.

What is the Great Ķemeri Bog boardwalk like? Is it a hike?

An on-boardwalk visit, not a hike. We stay on the wooden boards the whole time and walk a flat loop into the Great Ķemeri Bog, one of the largest and best-preserved raised bogs in the Baltics. From the boards you see sphagnum moss, dwarf pines that can be 200 or 300 years old and still no taller than a Christmas tree, and dozens of dark bog pools. No special equipment and no off-boardwalk walking.

Is this suitable for children?

Yes. The boardwalk loop at Ķemeri is flat and pram-friendly, Bauska Castle has tower stairs and wide river views, and Rundāle is slower-paced and about looking rather than touching. Tell us your children's ages when you book and Daiga plans the day with the group in mind. Child price is €70.

When are the Rundāle roses in bloom?

The French rose garden holds more than 2,300 varieties, with its main flush from mid-June through July and a strong second wave in late August and early September. Late June is the peak. May and early June give you the geometry of the beds with few flowers, and October gives you the last roses against the autumn light. The garden is included in your palace ticket and we always allow time for a slow walk through it.

What was the Duchy of Courland?

The Duchy of Courland and Semigallia, which existed from 1561 to 1795, was a small Baltic duchy that even briefly held overseas colonies in Tobago and the Gambia. Rundāle and Bauska were among its great monuments, and your guide brings that history to life across the day.

Is the palace accessible for reduced mobility?

Partly. The ground-floor state rooms at Rundāle, including the White Hall, are accessible, and that is where most of the visual highlights are. The formal gardens are flat gravel paths that work for most mobility aids in dry weather, and the Ķemeri boardwalk is flat wooden boards. The second-floor rooms need stairs and there is no lift, and Bauska Castle has cobblestones and a tower climb. Tell us when you book and we plan the day around you, skipping Bauska if you prefer and giving the palace or the bog more time instead.
