About the guide

Daiga Taurīte

Licensed Latvian tour guide. Co-founder of Barefoot Baltic. Writer of every guide on this site.

I’m Daiga — a licensed Latvian tour guide based in Riga. I founded Barefoot Baltic with my family in October 2024 to do something specific: small-group day excursions out of Riga, run honestly, by a Latvian, for travellers who want to actually understand the place rather than just photograph it. Maximum eight guests per departure. Often fewer.

If you have read any of the long pieces on the Barefoot Baltic blog — the room-by-room walkthrough of Rundāle Palace, the honest answers about Sigulda and Ḳemeri Bog, the comparison with Tallinn and Vilnius — you’ve been reading me. Every word, every photograph, every itinerary on this site comes from days I’ve actually run.

How I work

The idea behind Barefoot Baltic is simple: keep the groups small, keep the guide a Latvian, and keep the day honest. I don’t pad an itinerary with stops that don’t deserve your time. If a place isn’t worth a visit on a given day — the wrong season, the wrong weather, the wrong fit for the group — I’ll tell you, and we’ll do something better. That’s also the voice you’ll find in the blog: each piece is built around the question travellers actually ask, including the ones competing tour pages tend to dodge (“is this worth it?”, “who is this not for?”, “what should I skip?”).

I work with a tight, family-built operation. We use an air-conditioned minibus, your hotel pickup is included, all entrance fees are pre-paid, bottled water is on board, and the guide on the day is me — not a subcontractor.

“If a place isn’t worth a visit on a given day, I’ll tell you, and we’ll do something better.”

Languages

I personally guide in English and Latvian. Through Barefoot Baltic we also operate days in Russian, German, and French on request, subject to availability of the guide who covers that language. Email me before you book if a non-English-speaking guest joins you and I’ll confirm what we can offer for your dates.
