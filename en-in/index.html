Hello. I’m Daiga.

Thanks for stopping by. I am a licensed tour operator in Europe, and I run Barefoot Baltic together with my small team of tour leaders and drivers. We are proud to be a women-led company and welcoming of guests from diverse backgrounds.

I am married into an Indian family, visit India often and have a registered office there. I am particularly experienced at hosting Indian guests in Europe - visa requirements, food preferences, safety of solo travellers, etc.

My holiday packages aim to cover the top TripAdvisor attractions, UNESCO highlights and a few handpicked hidden gems. We cover themes including - Nature tours, Multi-city breaks, Light adventure and History / Heritage tours. I am able to organize private tours or customize existing routes. Kindly reach out to me if you have any questions.
